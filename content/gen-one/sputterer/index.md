## Target Specs

The goal of this machine is to be able to sputter aluminum metallization layers and bulk dielectric oxides.

## Timeline

Goal: sputterer done by Fri Mar 5, 2027.

| Phase | Dates | Outcome |

| Parts Delivery | Mon Dec 14 – Fri Dec 25 | Parts received |

### Purchasing and Design - Nov 16 – Dec 11
- **Week of 11/16:** get quotes and lead times for the pumps, gauges and PSU. Submit purchase and funding requests for the pumps, gauges, chamber and PSU.
- **Week of 11/23:** order the pumps, gauges, PSU, cube chamber and Al target. Shrink the magnetron CAD to v2 based on Prof. Cho's review and 3D print it to check the fit.
- **Week of 11/30:** COMSOL simulation of the v2 magnet orientations. Order the magnets. Add the water cooling parts and dark space shield to the CAD.
- **Week of 12/7:** design review with advisors. Machining drawings for the magnetron parts. Machine the basic parts and assemble the chamber.

### Parts Delivery - Dec 14 – Dec 25
- **Week of 12/14:** receive the parts and check them against the BOM.
- **Week of 12/21:** winter break, buffer for late shipments.

### Pump and Safety Prep - Dec 28 – Jan 8
- **Week of 12/28:** test the scroll and turbo pumps on a blank flange and log the pump-down.
- **Week of 1/4:** wiring and interlock diagram for the PSU, pumps, gauges and HV interlock. Safety SOP for HV, implosion, startup and shutdown.

### Build - Jan 11 – Feb 12
- **Week of 1/11:** machine the magnetron parts.
- **Week of 1/18:** assemble the magnetron. Check for water leaks and compare gaussmeter readings to the COMSOL simulation.
- **Week of 1/25:** assemble the chamber and magnetron.
- **Week of 2/1:** pump down and leak check.
- **Week of 2/8:** install the magnetron, argon line, Ar flow control and PSU wiring.

### Testing - Feb 15 – Mar 5
- **Week of 2/15:** safety check with the advisor. First plasma at a few mTorr Ar.
- **Week of 2/22:** first Al deposition on a glass slide. Measure conductivity and film thickness.
- **Week of 3/1:** buffer and fixes.

Next: add the RF power supply for dielectric deposition.
