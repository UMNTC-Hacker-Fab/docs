## Weekly update #1 (11/24/26)

I worked on making the safety documentation for the chemicals and safety equipment. 

PPE required: goggles, nitrile gloves, lab coat 
non-flammable clothing (natural fibers: cotton, denim, wool - avoid polyester/nylon), close-toed shoes, organic breathing mask (better alternative is fume hood)

Ethanol and TEOS must be stored in a flammable storage cabinet and labeled. 

Reflux apparatus + stir rod + heating mantle - ideas to do without heating mantle/control at a safe temperature <flash point or <<boiling temperature
TEOS can form glass when reacted with water in the air, so vapors must be kept away from eyes/lungs. Only work under chemical fume hood and keep working volume small <50 ml

Chemical disposal required (still working on specifics for UMN)
